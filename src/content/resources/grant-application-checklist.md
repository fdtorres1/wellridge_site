Use this checklist to organize an identified opportunity before you spend time drafting. It is a starting point, not a substitute for the controlling funder instructions, organizational policy, or accountable human review.

## Opportunity and fit

- [ ] Funder, program, and application URL are identified.
- [ ] Current deadline, time zone, submission method, and any notice-of-intent date are confirmed.
- [ ] Eligibility, geography, program restrictions, and required match or other support are checked.
- [ ] The organization has named an accountable decision-maker and final submitter.

## Program and evidence

- [ ] The proposed activity is an existing approved program or its new-design status is explicitly scoped.
- [ ] Population, place, timing, activities, outputs, and outcome targets are specific.
- [ ] Each important claim has a source, internal owner, or clear qualification.
- [ ] Measurement method, cadence, and limitations are stated without promising results that do not exist.

## Budget and attachments

- [ ] The budget total reconciles to the narrative and workplan.
- [ ] Request amount, committed support, and planned but unsecured support are labeled separately.
- [ ] Cost basis, time period, and any match or indirect-cost treatment are documented.
- [ ] Required attachments have owners, current versions, and enough lead time for approval.

## Final review

- [ ] The narrative answers each scored criterion in the funder's order or an easy-to-follow crosswalk.
- [ ] Names, dates, totals, and organizational facts match across every file.
- [ ] The client has approved the facts and final package.
- [ ] The client—not an outside writing support provider—retains submission authority.
