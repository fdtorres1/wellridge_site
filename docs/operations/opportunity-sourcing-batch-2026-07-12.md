## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — What public sourcing can and cannot do

Public research can identify organizations at a plausible decision or implementation moment. It cannot verify a warm path, internal budget availability, buyer preference, permissible contractor use, or actual capacity gap. The first five records should therefore move next into deeper local qualification and relationship mapping—not into automatic outreach.
