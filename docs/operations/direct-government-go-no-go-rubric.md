# Direct-government opportunity go/no-go rubric

Last updated: July 12, 2026

**Status: DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Use mandatory gates before weighted scoring. No numerical score can override a failed eligibility, proof, capability, ethics, contract, cash, or delivery gate.**

This rubric decides whether Wellridge should consider a named direct-government pursuit. It is separate from the preliminary account-research score in the [opportunity-sourcing playbook](opportunity-sourcing-playbook.md):

- the account score ranks where additional research may be useful;
- this rubric tests an actual forecast or solicitation; and
- neither score authorizes outreach, registration, attendance, teaming, or submission.

This is an internal planning control, not legal, procurement, tax, insurance, security, clinical, engineering, or accounting advice. Contract-specific requirements require qualified human review.

Wellridge Partners, LLC is not currently formed, cleared, insured, registered, certified, or approved to bid. Until those facts change and Felix explicitly authorizes a pursuit, an otherwise attractive live opportunity remains `HOLD` or `NO-BID` for direct submission.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Controlled labels

### Opportunity stage

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FORECAST / PRE-RELEASE` | An authoritative source predicts a procurement, but the controlling solicitation is unavailable. |
| `OPEN` | The buyer's designated source shows a current solicitation and controlling deadline. |
| `CLOSED` | The submission deadline has passed or the buyer reports no opening. Use only for learning. |
| `UNVERIFIED` | A lead exists, but current status cannot be confirmed in the controlling source. |

### Pursuit posture

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DIRECT-BID CANDIDATE` | All hard gates pass and the weighted score supports a solo-prime review. Felix must still authorize pursuit. |
| `TEAM-ONLY` | The opportunity contains a bounded, material Wellridge work package, but another qualified firm must own a central credential or technical workstream. |
| `WATCH / PRE-CAPTURE` | Timing or facts are incomplete; monitor and prepare internally without representing interest. |
| `HOLD` | Fit may exist, but a potentially resolvable formation, proof, capacity, terms, conflict, or authorization gate prevents action. |
| `NO-BID` | A hard gate fails, the score is too weak, the central competence sits elsewhere, or the burden is strategically unsuitable. |

`TEAM-ONLY` is not permission to contact a firm. It describes the structure that would be required if Felix later authorizes partner development.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Step 1: source and timing control

Before assessing fit:

1. Locate the buyer's official procurement page and designated submission portal.
2. Save the solicitation, attachments, forms, addenda, Q&A, event notices, and current deadline.
3. Record the access date and time, solicitation number, issuing office, buyer contact channel, and controlling document order.
4. Distinguish an official forecast from an open competition.
5. Treat a board budget, grant total, prior contract spend, or overall program value as context—not the current contract ceiling—unless the solicitation says otherwise.
6. Record contradictions and use the formal question process if Felix later authorizes pursuit; do not silently select the convenient interpretation.

If status is `FORECAST / PRE-RELEASE`, `CLOSED`, or `UNVERIFIED`, do not call the opportunity a live bid. Stop at `WATCH / PRE-CAPTURE`, learning, or archive.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Step 2: mandatory gates

Rate every gate `PASS`, `CONDITIONAL`, `FAIL`, or `UNKNOWN` and cite the controlling requirement.

| Gate | `PASS` requires | Override rule |
|---|---|---|
| Live and usable timing | Officially open, enough working days remain, required events/questions are feasible, and every addendum can be controlled | Closed or impossibly compressed work is `NO-BID`; forecast/unknown is `WATCH` |
| Entity and procurement eligibility | Entity, tax form, registrations, representations, debarment checks, certifications actually required, and signature authority can truthfully be satisfied by the deadline | Do not imply readiness; unresolved mandatory eligibility is `HOLD` or `NO-BID` |
| Central owned scope | Wellridge can own the core result through administration, engagement, proposal, implementation, coordination, reporting, or nonclinical program operations | If another party would own the central competence, use `TEAM-ONLY` or `NO-BID`, never cosmetic priming |
| Mandatory proof | Every minimum reference, similar-project, resume, license, credential, portfolio, and past-performance requirement is substantiated, attributable, contactable, and permission-ready | An unfillable minimum is `NO-BID`; do not invent or relabel experience |
| Specialist and team completeness | Any bounded technical, clinical, engineering, data, creative, or legal work has a qualified owner and compliant workshare | Missing material expertise is `TEAM-ONLY`, `HOLD`, or `NO-BID` |
| Contract and compliance acceptability | Insurance, indemnity, records, audit, security, privacy, IP, flow-down, subcontract, public-record, and acceptance terms are understood and feasible | Unreviewed high-risk terms or prohibited obligations prevent a direct bid |
| Delivery capacity and founder fit | Schedule, meetings, travel, fieldwork, staffing, revision control, QA, and deadline ownership fit actual capacity | Do not bid on a plan that assumes Felix can carry every detail or exceed the active-project cap |
| Economics and liquidity | Price can cover labor, partners, contingency, insurance, overhead, payment lag, and cash exposure while meeting the applicable provisional contribution target/floor | Unfunded pass-throughs, negative cash exposure, or inadequate margin are `NO-BID` |
| Ethics, conflicts, and claim integrity | No improper influence, contingent-fee problem, related-party conflict, clinical boundary violation, or unsupported representation exists | Any unresolved integrity problem is `NO-BID` |

### Gate decision rule

- Any `FAIL` is `NO-BID`, except a central specialist gap may become `TEAM-ONLY` when a qualified partner can own that bounded work without making Wellridge a pass-through prime.
- Any `UNKNOWN` on a mandatory requirement prevents `DIRECT-BID CANDIDATE` status.
- `CONDITIONAL` is allowed only when the evidence, owner, deadline, and resolution path are recorded and feasible before the internal decision deadline.
- Formation, insurance purchase, registration, outreach, and teaming are external actions and require separate authorization; a plan to perform them is not the same as a passed gate.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Step 3: weighted pursuit score

Score only after the mandatory gates pass or after a clearly bounded specialist gap establishes a legitimate `TEAM-ONLY` structure.

Rate each dimension from 0 to 4:

| Rating | Evidence standard |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Absent, contradicted, or materially outside capability |
| 1 | Weak, largely inferred, or unlikely to be competitive |
| 2 | Credible but incomplete, ordinary, or dependent on constraints |
| 3 | Strong, substantiated, and responsive to the buyer's stated priorities |
| 4 | Clear advantage supported by directly relevant proof and a realistic delivery plan |

Weighted points equal `rating / 4 × weight`.

| Dimension | Weight | What to test |
|---|---:|---|
| Past performance and buyer-specific proof | 25 | Comparable references, results, resumes, artifacts, public/funded-program experience, and permission to use them |
| Owned-scope fit | 20 | Share and centrality of work Wellridge can responsibly lead, plus documented value beyond coordination or pass-through |
| Buyer and program-domain fit | 15 | Understanding of the agency, funding environment, stakeholders, service population, and operating problem |
| Delivery method and capacity | 15 | Credible approach, workplan, staffing, partner controls, location/travel fit, QA, reporting, and schedule resilience |
| Margin, cash flow, and contract risk | 15 | Defensible price, retained gross profit, payment timing, vendor exposure, insurance, clauses, data, and acceptance risk |
| Strategic value and reusable proof | 10 | Reference potential, repeat/task-order path, method reuse, niche evidence, and relationship value without concentration risk |
| **Total** | **100** | A score is not a win probability or revenue forecast |

### Score interpretation

| Score | Default posture, assuming all mandatory gates pass |
|---:|---|
| 75–100 | `DIRECT-BID CANDIDATE` |
| 55–74 | `TEAM-ONLY` or `HOLD`; document and resolve the conditions before pursuit |
| 0–54 | `NO-BID` |

Use the lower posture when the facts sit near a boundary. A score above 75 does not cure a failed gate, and a strong scope fit does not cure missing references.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Step 4: decision record

Every reviewed opportunity should end with a one-page record containing:

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Opportunity identity | Buyer, solicitation name/number, official URL, stage, access date/time |
| Controlling dates | Release, questions, conference, addenda, submission, anticipated award |
| Scope map | Wellridge-owned, shared, partner-owned, prohibited, and ambiguous tasks |
| Mandatory gates | Rating, evidence, owner, resolution date, and source for every gate |
| Score | Six component ratings, weighted calculation, total, and caveats |
| Economics | Contract ceiling if disclosed, Wellridge revenue, pass-through, direct cost, gross profit, cash timing, and downside exposure kept separate |
| Recommended posture | `DIRECT-BID CANDIDATE`, `TEAM-ONLY`, `WATCH / PRE-CAPTURE`, `HOLD`, or `NO-BID` |
| Decision authority | Felix's dated approval or rejection; absence of approval means no pursuit |
| Next action | Local research, authorized formal question, authorized partner step, archive, or no action |

Do not count overall program funding, submitted proposal value, pass-through vendor spend, recognized revenue, cash collected, owner compensation, or true business profit as interchangeable figures.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Rubric calibration

The 75/55 score bands and six weights are operating hypotheses. Recalibrate after at least five serious direct-government reviews using reviewer disagreement, no-bid reasons, buyer feedback where ethically obtained, proposal results, delivery margin, and founder time. Do not lower a hard gate merely to create more pursuits.

## OPEN QUESTION

- Do actual direct-government workshare economics support the provisional contribution guardrails, and what maximum cash-at-risk should become a hard numeric gate?
- Which personal or prior-employer examples may be attributed to Felix, with what wording and permission?
- Which three future references could satisfy common public/nonprofit similar-project requirements?
- What insurance, data-security, legal-review, and active-project-cap thresholds must be met before any direct bid?
- Which qualified technical and clinical partner archetypes should be researched locally before outreach is authorized?
- Where should signed-off decision records live once a CRM or opportunity control plane is selected?

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED

- Scoring a forecast as though it were a released RFP
- Treating a high score as outreach or submission authority
- Bidding before mandatory formation, eligibility, insurance, proof, or contract gates are satisfied
- Using subcontractors to disguise the absence of the prime's central competence
- Generic “prime anything” pursuits
- Unsupported references, credentials, certifications, results, or domain claims
- Clinical, engineering, construction, software-development, or other technical judgment Wellridge cannot own
- Large pass-through or event/media buys without retained margin, working capital, and contract protection
- Pursuing a deadline because it exists rather than because Wellridge has a credible right to win and deliver
