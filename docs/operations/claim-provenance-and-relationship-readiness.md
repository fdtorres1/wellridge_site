## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Claim readiness states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SELF_REPORTED_INPUT` | Felix or a strategy document states the fact; no sufficient artifact has been reviewed |
| `ARTIFACT_LOCATED` | A relevant source/work sample exists and its ownership, scope, and attribution have been checked |
| `PERMISSION_CONFIRMED` | Required client/employer/partner/reference permission and confidentiality review are documented |
| `PUBLICLY_USABLE` | Accountable human review approves exact wording, context, attribution, date, and channel |
| `QUARANTINED` | Claim is unsupported, misleading, conflicted, outdated, or inappropriate for current use |

Progression is claim-specific. An artifact does not automatically grant permission, and permission does not make wording accurate or legally safe. These are operational readiness states, not substitutes for the four required decision-status labels; the matrix records both separately.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Relationship-route states

Relationship status is separate from procurement access and from an account's sourcing lifecycle.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VERIFIED_FORMAL_ROUTE` | An official supplier, procurement, or registry path is evidenced; it is not a relationship, demand signal, or permission to register |
| `PLAUSIBLE_PUBLIC_ROUTE` | Public contact, ecosystem adjacency, or membership may support future research; no permissioned introduction exists |
| `UNKNOWN` | No usable route is documented |
| `VERIFIED_WARM_PATH` | Felix confirms a specific person-level relationship, basis, recency, conflict check, and ethical permission/context for an introduction or contact |

Do not store personal contact details, sensitive relationship notes, or confidential history in this repository. The future secure CRM may store them; this register records only readiness state.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Secure relationship inventory schema

If Felix authorizes a relationship-mapping task, use a secure, access-controlled system rather than this public repository. Minimum fields:

- account ID and organization;
- person and role;
- relationship basis and how Felix knows them;
- last meaningful interaction date;
- whether business contact is expected/appropriate;
- conflicts, confidentiality, employer, grant, procurement, or related-party restrictions;
- permission/introduction status;
- likely problem or mutual value, clearly labeled as hypothesis;
- accountable next action and owner; and
- source of the information and last review date.
