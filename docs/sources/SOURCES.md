- Type: authoritative regulation

<a id="s07"></a>
### S07 — FAR 52.215-23, limitations on pass-through charges

- Type: authoritative regulation
- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.215-23

- Caveat: clause applicability is contract-specific; listed value-add functions are not a general safe harbor.

- Caveat: applies to specified qualifying contracts and requests; preserve evidence and verify eligibility.

<a id="s09"></a>
### S09 — FAR 32.904, payment due dates

- Type: authoritative regulation
- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/32.904
- Supports: general invoice-payment due date as the later of 30 days after proper invoice receipt or 30 days after acceptance, subject to exceptions.
- Caveat: contract, invoice, acceptance, and exception terms control.

<a id="s10"></a>
### S10 — FAR 32.009-1, accelerated payment

- Type: authoritative regulation
- URL: https://www.acquisition.gov/far/32.009-1
- Supports: 15-day accelerated-payment goal for qualifying small-business situations; for the relevant prime path, payment to the small-business subcontractor within 15 days after accelerated government receipt and after proper subcontractor invoice/documentation, without added consideration or fees; absence of a new Prompt Payment Act right.
- Caveat: does not guarantee 15-day operating cash receipt.

<a id="s11"></a>
### S11 — 13 CFR 121.104, SBA receipts

- Type: authoritative regulation
- URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/chapter-I/part-121/section-121.104
- Supports: SBA size-calculation receipts generally include subcontractor costs and customer-requested purchase reimbursements.
- Caveat: SBA receipts are not automatically GAAP, tax, or financial-statement revenue.
