# Wellridge source-of-truth documentation

**Status: DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — This directory is the internal planning source of truth as of July 12, 2026.**

The Astro source in `src/` now implements a local **Wellridge Partners** migration using the user-designated `wellridgepartners.com` domain while preserving the existing visual design system. The migration has not been deployed, and no contact or external integration is active. The working strategy remains for **Wellridge Partners, LLC**, a company that has not yet been legally formed, cleared, registered, certified, insured, or launched.

These documents are planning materials. They are not legal, tax, accounting, clinical, procurement, or investment advice. Time-sensitive claims must be rechecked before use. AI may assist research and drafting, but a qualified human must review and approve external-facing, legal, clinical, financial, and contract-specific work.

## Status labels

Every material planning item uses one of these labels:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DECIDED / WORKING DECISION` | Current direction or source-backed planning input accepted for use now. It may still change after validation or professional review. |
| `HYPOTHESIS TO TEST` | Forecast, price, probability, interpretation, or operating assumption requiring market or delivery evidence. |
| `OPEN QUESTION` | A choice or fact that is not yet resolved. |
| `EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED` | Not part of the current company model or not authorized in the present task. |

Source-backed research is dated. Calling a source a `DECIDED / WORKING DECISION` means only that the documented finding is accepted as a planning input on the stated access date; it does not turn a changing rule or market fact into a permanent decision.

## Change discipline

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Keep planning, decisions, current context, work history, and evidence separate.** Update the strategy document that owns a topic, record a material decision in `DECISION_LOG.md`, append work to `WORKLOG.md`, and refresh `ACTIVE_CONTEXT.md` when the handoff changes.

**EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED — Documentation does not authorize execution.** A local public-copy migration was separately authorized on July 12, 2026; that authorization did not include filing, purchase, registration, outreach, submission, deployment, DNS changes, or external-system activation.
