# CroSS-DFW proof inventory

Last updated: July 12, 2026

**Status: OPEN QUESTION — A minimal local discovery pass found candidate founder-history and prior-work artifacts, but no CroSS-ready corporate past performance, permission-ready reference set, or approved experience claim.**

This inventory identifies what evidence would be needed to support a future CroSS-DFW team role. It does not upgrade a strategy statement, local website sentence, or Felix's self-reported background into verified past performance.

This is internal planning, not legal, procurement, employment, privacy, or public-claims advice. Protected work product, confidential relationship details, PII, PHI, credentials, and reference contact information do not belong in this repository.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Proof-control rules

1. Keep **Wellridge firm experience**, **Felix's individual experience**, and **a partner's experience** separate.
2. Attribute each project to the organization that held the contract, grant, employment relationship, or work-product rights.
3. Do not imply that an unformed firm performed prior work.
4. Do not use a client, employer, funder, agency, project, result, logo, testimonial, or reference without documented authority and permission.
5. A located artifact proves only the facts visible in that artifact; it does not prove authorship, ownership, causation, or permission.
6. Use the readiness states in the [claim-provenance register](../../operations/claim-provenance-and-relationship-readiness.md): `SELF_REPORTED_INPUT`, `ARTIFACT_LOCATED`, `PERMISSION_CONFIRMED`, `PUBLICLY_USABLE`, or `QUARANTINED`.
7. Any solicitation-specific reference, recency, contract-size, client-type, or project-similarity rule controls after release.
8. Keep discovery state separate from claim readiness. A candidate that is merely found or minimally screened has not reached `ARTIFACT_LOCATED` until ownership, scope, and attribution are checked.
