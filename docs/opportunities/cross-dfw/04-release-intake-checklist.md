## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Entry labels

Record two separate fields for each material entry.

### Decision status

- `DECIDED / WORKING DECISION` — a dated planning input or human decision accepted for current use.
- `OPEN QUESTION` — unresolved, contradictory, unclear, inaccessible, or not yet verified; never fill by inference.
- `HYPOTHESIS TO TEST` — a proposed interpretation, workshare, cost, score, or competitive judgment requiring validation.
- `EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED` — a human-approved boundary, failed hard gate, or no-bid reason.

### Evidence type

- `SOURCE FACT` — stated in an authoritative source; record document, section/page, URL, and access time.
- `HUMAN DECISION` — explicitly decided by the named accountable person on a recorded date.
- `ANALYTIC INFERENCE` — derived from source facts but not stated by the buyer; ordinarily pair with `HYPOTHESIS TO TEST`.
- `UNKNOWN` — missing or not verified; ordinarily pair with `OPEN QUESTION`.

Evidence type does not replace decision status. Operational gate ratings such as `PASS`, `CONDITIONAL`, `FAIL`, and `UNKNOWN` are a third, separate control.
