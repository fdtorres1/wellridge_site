# CroSS-DFW workshare map

Last updated: July 12, 2026

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — This is a pre-release team-design model. Current posture remains `WATCH / PRE-CAPTURE`, with `TEAM-ONLY` assumed unless the controlling solicitation materially changes the preliminary scope.**

No row creates a team, assigns a real firm, establishes a credential, or authorizes pursuit. A qualified transportation-planning and safety firm is assumed to hold prime or technical-lead authority; Wellridge's potential role is a bounded stakeholder-engagement and program-implementation workstream.

This is internal planning, not engineering, safety, legal, procurement, data, insurance, or accounting advice. The released RFP and qualified human reviewers control.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Required handoff controls

1. **One source register:** every technical assertion traces to validated data, source-tagged stakeholder evidence, approved specialist analysis, or NCTCOG direction.
2. **Source-tagged engagement:** distinguish observed fact, participant opinion, requested change, unresolved conflict, and technical question.
3. **Locked technical content:** after specialist approval, Wellridge may improve organization and plain language but cannot alter thresholds, conclusions, limitations, or safety meaning without re-review.
4. **Two-stage QA:** Wellridge performs completeness, consistency, accessibility, cross-reference, and production QA; the accountable specialist performs technical QA.
5. **Named decision rights:** only the appropriate technical lead closes technical comments. Wellridge routes and tracks; it does not adjudicate.
6. **Client release control:** nothing public-facing is distributed before NCTCOG approval through the contractually designated process.
7. **Field authority:** the technical/field lead controls installation, testing, field safety, and stop-work decisions.
8. **Change control:** any change affecting sites, sampling, technical criteria, equipment, evaluation, safety conclusions, cash exposure, or the critical path requires documented lead and client approval.
9. **Financial separation:** track total contract value, Wellridge revenue, specialist revenue, pass-through, direct cost, retained contribution, cash timing, and owner labor separately.
10. **Authority-based closeout:** each specialist certifies its scope; the prime makes contractual representations; Wellridge compiles the evidence archive.

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED — Red lines

- Cosmetic priming while technical firms own the central result
- Wellridge originating, approving, signing, sealing, or defending transportation-safety, engineering, statistical, data, or technology conclusions
- Wellridge selecting sites, crossing-guard placement criteria, safety measures, technical priorities, or demonstration products
- Wellridge directing traffic control, field installation, testing, or stop-work decisions
- Unsupported technology-effectiveness or student-safety claims
- Student PII or other sensitive data without a necessary, explicitly compliant purpose and adequate controls
- Material hardware, installation, or subcontractor costs fronted without protected cash flow
- Equipment ownership, warranties, maintenance, field liability, or uncontrolled indemnity
- Certification of partner cost, credentials, completion, or compliance without direct evidence and authority
- Unlimited coordination, revision, meeting, travel, or public-response burden
- Unapproved prior-employer experience, unsupported references, or formed-entity representations
- Pursuit without Felix's explicit authorization after the released packet is reviewed
