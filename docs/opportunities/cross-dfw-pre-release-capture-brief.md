## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Program logic

The official project materials describe a regional effort to improve student safety near high-injury roads by coordinating crossing-guard practices, school-zone safety analysis, stakeholder input, and driver-awareness technology.

Expected work and outcomes include:

- documenting current school-zone and crossing-guard conditions, practices, gaps, and safety concerns;
- conducting school, ISD, municipal, and other stakeholder outreach;
- creating regional recommendations and a crossing-guard framework addressing placement, staffing, management, and complementary measures;
- improving coordination between cities, ISDs, schools, and regional partners;
- demonstrating driver-awareness technology and measuring before-and-after conditions; and
- producing data and lessons that support the region's broader pedestrian and roadway-safety work.

The February presentation describes a preliminary study universe of approximately **200–300 K–8 school zones** near identified high-injury corridors or in rural areas. That estimate remains preliminary until the released solicitation controls.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Scope Wellridge may and may not own

### Potential Wellridge-owned scope

- engagement planning and stakeholder operations;
- school, ISD, city, and partner coordination;
- interview, meeting, and convening logistics;
- implementation cadence, roles, governance, and workflow design;
- workplans, compliance and deliverable calendars, issue logs, and evidence files;
- milestone reporting, document production, and quality control;
- translation of technically approved conclusions into implementation guidance and public-education materials; and
- subcontractor coordination where contract terms, capacity, and cash protection are adequate.

### Technical-partner-owned scope

- transportation planning, traffic engineering, and safety judgment;
- site-selection methodology and school-zone existing-conditions analysis;
- crash, speed, roadway, traffic, GIS, and statistical analysis;
- crossing-guard placement or other technical safety criteria;
- warning-technology selection, design, equipment specification, installation, integration, and testing;
- experimental design and before-and-after effectiveness analysis;
- sealed engineering work or conclusions governed by professional standards; and
- any technical statement that could materially affect public safety.

**EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED — Wellridge will not present itself as a transportation planner, traffic engineer, technology integrator, or school-zone safety authority.**

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Release-stage hard gates

When an official solicitation appears, use the [direct-government go/no-go rubric](../operations/direct-government-go-no-go-rubric.md). The opportunity cannot move beyond `WATCH / PRE-CAPTURE` until the official packet is available.

At minimum, verify:

1. official solicitation number, release date, deadline, addenda, pre-proposal events, and controlling portal;
2. consultant ceiling, term, payment structure, and whether demonstration equipment or vendors are pass-through costs;
3. exact evaluation weights and whether engagement, implementation, and program management are material scored work;
4. mandatory planning, engineering, data, technology, DBE, reference, and past-performance requirements;
5. permitted teaming structure, subcontracting rules, joint-venture rules, and required workshare disclosures;
6. federal SS4A clauses, records rules, flow-downs, insurance, indemnity, security, and audit requirements;
7. the sampling method and expected number of school-zone assessments;
8. whether hardware ownership, installation, maintenance, and demonstration risk sit with the consultant; and
9. whether Wellridge can own a bounded, meaningful, profitable work package without controlling technical judgment.

Any missing mandatory credential, unfillable reference requirement, central technical-capability gap, or unprotected pass-through obligation overrides strategic enthusiasm and produces `TEAM-ONLY`, `HOLD`, or `NO-BID`.
