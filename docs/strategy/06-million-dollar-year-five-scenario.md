# Aggressive $1 million Year-5 scenario

Last updated: July 11, 2026

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Aggressive target case, not the baseline, promise, or probability-weighted forecast.**

“$1 million Year 5” means **$1 million of recognized revenue during calendar 2031**. It does not mean $1 million cumulative revenue and does not mean a December annualized run rate.

For comparison, the conservative launch period plus Years 1–5 totals approximately **$913,000 cumulatively** (`18 + 75 + 115 + 165 + 230 + 310`). Cumulative revenue and single-year revenue must never be interchanged.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Required revenue staircase

| Period | Target recognized revenue | Approximate organization |
|---|---:|---|
| Jul–Dec 2026 | $25,000–$30,000 | Felix + project contractors |
| 2027 / Y1 | $125,000 | Felix + half-time coordinator |
| 2028 / Y2 | $250,000 | Full-time manager + part-time research |
| 2029 / Y3 | $450,000 | Three delivery staff + fractional capture/admin |
| 2030 / Y4 | $700,000 | Five to six staff, including dedicated capture |
| 2031 / Y5 | $1,000,000 | Six to eight non-founder FTE equivalents + specialist bench |

Missing a major annual milestone may move the $1 million point back one or two years. The response to a miss should be reforecasting, not forcing volume or low-margin work.

The illustration totals approximately **$1.002 million** because of rounding. The strategic conclusion is more important than the exact mix: four large program contracts or equivalent anchor work are essential. More small grant sprints alone do not create the required scale.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Year-5 pipeline requirements

- approximately 50–60% repeat, renewal, or task-order revenue;
- approximately $400,000–$500,000 genuinely new annual revenue;
- approximately $1.5 million–$2 million submitted proposal value annually;
- approximately $3 million–$5 million qualified opportunity pipeline;
- approximately 15–25 serious pursuits annually; and
- approximately 25–35% win rate on properly qualified pursuits.

These are operating targets, not guaranteed benchmarks. “Qualified” must mean buyer, need, budget/path, timing, fit, decision access, competition, capacity, margin, and next action are credible.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION:** this path requires a dedicated capture function. It does not require a generic high-volume SDR operation, but it does require systematic relationship management among a concentrated set of primes, agencies, nonprofits, funders, and partners.

If Felix owns 100%, illustrative pre-tax owner economic benefit is:

Distributions still depend on cash, taxes, debt, reserves, working-capital needs, and legal/accounting decisions. A $1 million contract book containing $700,000 of pass-through vendor spend would have dramatically weaker economics. Track gross billings and retained gross profit separately.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Founder constraint

Felix cannot simultaneously remain near 15 hours/week from the beginning, avoid sales, lack capital, and reach $1 million by Year 5.

At least one condition must change:

1. Felix works approximately 25–30 focused hours/week during the first one to two years, then reduces time as staff takes over; or
2. the company secures enough capital to hire senior program and capture capacity materially earlier.

A 15–20-hour strategic founder role becomes plausible in Years 4–5 only if the organization is actually built.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Probability framing

Judgment estimates, not published base rates:

- Current low-capital, 12–25-hour, founder-reluctant-to-sell model: approximately **8–15%** chance of $1 million 2031 revenue
- With the staircase, retained earnings, operator, and dedicated capture: approximately **20–30%**
- Conditional on $250,000 in 2028 with two anchor program clients: approximately **35–50%**
- If Felix stays near 15 hours/week, adds no capture function, and relies on one-off sprints: **below 5%**

## OPEN QUESTION — Moonshot decisions

- Whether Felix wants the workload, reinvestment, sales responsibility, and organizational complexity required
- How much capital can be risked and for how long
- Which anchor program family can support $100,000+ contracts with acceptable retained margin
- When a capture leader becomes affordable and credible
- Whether the required non-founder leadership can be recruited without overcommitting payroll
- Client-concentration and downside limits for four anchor contracts

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED

- Treating $1 million as the baseline or public promise
- Reaching the target through low-value pass-through billings
- Forcing small-project volume beyond Felix and the delivery system’s capacity
- Hiring to the staircase without collections, backlog, pipeline, and runway evidence
- Confusing cumulative revenue, run rate, bookings, contract ceiling, and recognized Year-5 revenue
