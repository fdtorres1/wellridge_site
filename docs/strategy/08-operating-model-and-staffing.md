# Operating model and staffing

Last updated: July 12, 2026

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Design around the founder

The operating design uses founder-supplied experience, preference, and capacity inputs. Those inputs are valid for internal planning, but they are not automatically substantiated public claims. Claim-specific evidence, attribution, permission, and approval are controlled in the [claim-provenance and relationship-readiness register](../operations/claim-provenance-and-relationship-readiness.md).

### OPEN QUESTION — Founder advantages and provenance

The following remain `SELF_REPORTED_INPUT` until the evidence register supports a more advanced readiness state:

- More than a decade of operational and administrative leadership
- Nonprofit, grant, federal-application, reporting, event, education, and public-facing program experience
- Direct nonclinical behavioral-health operating knowledge
- Strong writing, narrative architecture, relationship-building, hosting, and executive orchestration
- Existing North Texas nonprofit and public-sector-adjacent relationships, with no verified warm path yet documented

Use these as internal design hypotheses. Do not convert them into exact tenure, client-result, clinical-authority, partner-network, or warm-relationship claims without the required provenance and permission.

### DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Founder constraints as design inputs

- ADHD, context-switching risk, and overload from detail tracking
- Strong dislike of repetitive administrative follow-through and cold sales
- Preference for fixed-scope, high-value, low-volume work
- Desired workload generally around 15–25 hours/week, with about 15 dependable hours in some weeks
- Preference for daytime, asynchronous, remote-friendly work and limited evenings/travel
- No desire to build a music-administration business or assume clinical credentials/responsibility

The operating system must treat these constraints as design inputs, not character flaws to overcome through willpower.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Intended Felix role

Felix owns:

- capture strategy and relationship development;
- opportunity judgment and go/no-go decisions;
- solution design;
- client interviews and high-level communication;
- narrative architecture;
- partner selection; and
- final quality control.

Felix should not remain the sole owner of:

- deadline memory;
- attachment and document chasing;
- production checklists;
- routine status follow-up;
- detailed reporting trackers;
- day-to-day vendor coordination;
- bookkeeping and invoicing.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — First critical support role

The first consistent human support is an unusually organized **compliance/project coordinator**. Its purpose is not generic assistance; it is to own the control plane:

- master calendar and dependencies;
- client/partner input tracker;
- compliance matrix maintenance;
- file naming, version control, and evidence records;
- meeting notes, actions, and routine follow-up;
- production checklist and submission/acceptance evidence;
- deliverable, report, and closeout calendars; and
- invoice milestone handoff to bookkeeping.

Build approximately 15–20% of project fees into research, coordination, and QA even before the role is consistently outsourced.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Early contractor roles to source

These are required role archetypes, not proof that a current bench, relationship, credential, availability, or subcontracting commitment exists. Each person or firm must clear the relevant qualification, conflict, contract, insurance, data, and client-approval gates before being represented as available.

| Role | Used for | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Research analyst | funder/opportunity research, source capture, profiles | Felix approves fit and pursuit priority. |
| Compliance/project coordinator | control plane, inputs, calendars, QA, records | Does not make unapproved legal/clinical interpretations. |
| Proposal editor/production specialist | editing, formatting, assembly, red-team support | Client and Felix retain final approval. |
| Designer/communications specialist | visual production, public materials | Used only when the SOW funds it. |
| Evaluator/data specialist | measurement design, data quality, dashboards | Data access and methodology must be contractually governed. |
| Event/logistics provider | venues, production, onsite operations | Only when scope, margin, insurance, and cash protection support it. |
| Licensed clinical subject-matter lead | clinical boundaries, protocols, judgment, supervision where required | Clinical authority remains with qualified licensed leadership, not Felix or AI. |

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Standard delivery control loop

1. **Qualify:** buyer, need, budget/path, timing, scope fit, proof, capacity, margin, data, partners, and next action.
2. **Go/no-go:** document decision, exclusions, assumptions, and owner.
3. **Scope:** deliverables, inputs, roles, dates, revisions, meetings, data, acceptance, payment, and change control.
4. **Kickoff:** confirm source of truth, communication, decision-makers, risks, and dependency owners.
5. **Control:** one master calendar, action register, evidence file, compliance matrix, and version owner.
6. **Review:** coordinator/producer check, specialist check where required, Felix final QA, client approval.
7. **Deliver/accept:** record delivery, acceptance, invoice trigger, and unresolved items.
8. **Close:** actual hours/cost/margin, defects, collection timing, lessons, templates, reference readiness, and next task.

The blank [pricing gate](../templates/internal-pricing-gate.md), [Funding Pipeline Sprint workbook](../templates/nonprofit-funding-pipeline-delivery-workbook.md), [proposal compliance matrix](../templates/proposal-compliance-matrix.md), and [program-launch compliance calendar](../templates/program-launch-compliance-calendar.md) implement parts of this loop. They are designed controls, not proof of successful delivery, current capacity, or specialist authority.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Initial active-work cap

Until a coordinator is consistently available, do not run more than:

- 1 deadline-intensive proposal sprint;
- 1 program-operations implementation task; and
- 2 pipeline sprints

at the same time. Count work as active from kickoff until client acceptance, not merely until draft delivery. Reduce the cap when deadlines overlap or client inputs are unreliable.

This cap is a proposed starting control, not a settled capacity fact. Replace it with measured cycle time, task load, Felix hours, and defect data.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Operating cadence

| Cadence | Purpose | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly pipeline review | qualify, advance, pause, or close opportunities | next action, owner, date, value, probability class, blocker |
| Weekly delivery review | surface schedule/input/compliance/cash risk | red/yellow/green project status and recovery action |
| Per-milestone QA | prevent late discovery of defects | signed checklist and approval evidence |
| Monthly financial review | separate bookings, revenue, cash, margin, and owner pay | scorecard and 90-day cash view |
| Quarterly strategy review | validate offers, channels, niche, staffing, and capital | decision-log updates and reforecast |

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — AI and human review

AI may support research, drafting, comparison, compliance checks, formatting, and production. It may not be represented as independently authoritative.

Human review is required for:

- facts, citations, claims, calculations, budgets, and final submissions;
- legal, tax, accounting, procurement, privacy, and security interpretations;
- clinical content, protocols, judgment, triage, or supervision;
- client-sensitive data and permission to use it;
- partner/subcontractor selection and conflicts; and
- final external approval.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Staffing gates

Use the financial triggers in the [conservative scenario](05-conservative-financial-scenario.md), plus these operating gates:

- recurring coordinator work is at least 10–12 useful hours/week;
- Felix is spending material time on recurring control tasks the role can own;
- the candidate can maintain precise records and escalate ambiguity;
- collected contribution supports the role and cash reserve after hiring; and
- supervision time is explicitly budgeted.

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED

- Felix as the human reminder system for every project
- A tool stack without named owners and operating cadence
- Delegating final legal, clinical, financial, or contract authority to AI
- Hiring because a forecast says a calendar year has arrived
- Adding unrelated venture work to keep staff busy
