# Services and pricing

Last updated: July 12, 2026

All prices, labor estimates, margins, and conversion expectations in this file are **HYPOTHESIS TO TEST** unless explicitly labeled otherwise. They are internal planning ranges, not approved public prices.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Launch architecture

Only two front-door offers should be highly visible. Expansion work follows diagnosis and a bounded statement of work.

## Offer 1 — Nonprofit Funding Pipeline Sprint

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST**

### Buyer outcome

A qualified, prioritized pursuit plan that distinguishes plausible funders from generic lists and identifies evidence gaps before proposal spend.

### Working deliverables

- 20–30 research-qualified funder profiles, which are not Wellridge `QUALIFIED_OPPORTUNITY` records
- evidence of giving to comparable organizations or programs
- fit, risk, and exclusion analysis
- typical grant sizes where available
- deadlines and contact information
- priority ranking
- 6- or 12-month pursuit calendar
- next actions and evidence gaps

### Working economics

| Item | Hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Initial/pilot fee | approximately $2,250 |
| Standard fee after proof | approximately $3,000–$4,000 |
| Outside research/tool cost | approximately $300–$800 |
| Contribution after outside delivery cost, before Felix labor and overhead | approximately 75–90% |
| Payment structure | 60% deposit / 40% on delivery |

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contribution guardrail:** use an 80% base target and 75% stress floor for Pipeline Sprint pricing. The range above remains an unvalidated planning range; the guardrail controls scope-specific estimating.

**OPEN QUESTION — The full pilot economics do not yet reconcile.** At a $2,250 fee, $800 of outside cost produces 64.4% contribution before Felix labor and overhead; at $3,000, it produces 73.3%. Both are below the provisional 75% stress floor. The draft 16–20-hour narrow-pilot labor model also exceeds the labor value available in several range combinations before contingency and overhead. Resolve this through a tighter scope/cost cap, higher fee, an explicitly approved learning exception, or measured delivery efficiency before quoting. See the [internal Offer 1 scope card](../offers/nonprofit-funding-pipeline-sprint-scope-card.md).

### Required scope controls

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION**

- Define geography, program family, funder types, exclusions, list size, source depth, calendar horizon, and one revision cycle.
- State that research is a dated opportunity screen, not a grant guarantee.
- Exclude proposal writing, funder cultivation, and ongoing calendar management unless separately scoped.
- Require verified client facts and internal decision-makers for final prioritization.

**Status: DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Buyer-facing category; each engagement must use a bounded SOW.**

### Proposal-sprint module

Possible deliverables:

- solicitation analysis and go/no-go memo;
- compliance matrix;
- proposal calendar;
- document and partner coordination;
- narrative/workplan development or editing;
- budget-to-scope alignment; and
- production and submission QA.

Working economics:

| Item | Hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Ordinary initial proposal operations | approximately $4,000–$8,000 |
| More complex state, multi-agency, or federal work | separately scoped; may exceed the ordinary range |
| Outside delivery cost | approximately $750–$2,000 |
| Contribution before Felix labor and overhead | approximately 65–85% |

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contribution guardrail:** use a 70% base target and 65% stress floor for Proposal Operations pricing.

**HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — More defensible segmentation:** roughly $3,000–$5,000 for tightly bounded compliance/editing/production and $6,000–$12,000 for substantive writing and coordination. Public award data rarely reveals private proposal-service pricing, so early scopes must test this segmentation.

### Program-launch/compliance module

Possible deliverables:

- implementation plan;
- roles, governance, and escalation map;
- compliance and deliverable calendar;
- spend and milestone tracker;
- reporting framework;
- stakeholder and partner plan;
- vendor-coordination system; and
- records and closeout structure.

Working economics:

| Item | Hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Initial narrow task | approximately $10,000–$25,000 |
| Mature/larger task | approximately $30,000–$40,000+ |
| Outside delivery cost on initial tasks | approximately $3,000–$10,000 |
| Contribution before Felix labor and overhead | approximately 45–70% |

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contribution guardrail:** use a 55% base target and 50% stress floor for Program Launch pricing.

**OPEN QUESTION — Range endpoints do not all satisfy the provisional guardrails.** A $4,000 proposal scope with $2,000 of outside cost produces 50%, below the provisional 65% Proposal stress floor; a $10,000 program task with $10,000 of outside cost produces no direct contribution, below the provisional 50% Program stress floor. Task-specific estimating must control the fee/cost combination, labor, cash exposure, and contract terms. Direct-client bands also do not automatically transfer to white-label consultant or prime-subcontract work. See the [internal Offer 2 scope card](../offers/proposal-and-program-operations-support-scope-card.md).

### Required scope controls

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION**

- Specify owned work, client inputs, partner inputs, approvals, submission authority, revision cycles, meetings, travel, data, records, and closeout.
- Separate writing, production, capture, subject-matter work, implementation, and ongoing operations.
- Make clinical, legal, engineering, cybersecurity, accounting, and other specialist responsibilities explicit.
- Do not promise an award, approval, compliance outcome, or uninterrupted service.
- Require human review and final client approval of AI-assisted work.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Pricing method

Use an internal estimate, then quote a fixed fee or milestone schedule:

```text
estimated labor value
+ outside delivery and tool cost
+ risk/capital allowance where required
= proposed fixed fee
```

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contingency and stress method:** calculate planned labor value from role hours after applying 20% contingency once at the role level. Do not add another 20% fee line. Require a named stress scenario for every scope; when credible evidence is unavailable, use stress hours of at least 125% of the contingency-loaded plan. Use stress as an internal viability test against the $110 realization floor, contribution, economic-surplus, capacity, and cash gates—not as an automatic client charge.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional overhead proxies:** allocate 16% of professional-service fee in the base case and 25% in stress. Exclude pass-through and anything already recorded as direct project cost, fixed-staff allocation, Felix replacement labor, or risk allowance. Treat stress as an internal sensitivity, not an automatic fee line or worst-case company-overhead forecast. Replace or segment the proxies when trailing actual overhead, recognized revenue, and productive-capacity data are credible.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional project-economic-surplus references:** calculate `professional-service fee - direct delivery cost - fixed-staff allocation - Felix replacement labor - overhead - risk allowance`. Require at least 10% of professional-service fee in the base case and at least zero in stress. Reprice or rescope below either reference unless Felix approves a named learning exception. This is an internal pricing proxy, not accounting profit, cash, taxable income, or distributable funds.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Primary realization definition:** use `professional-service fee / total effective hours`. Professional-service fee excludes pass-through. Total effective hours include discovery, research, production, meetings, coordination, revisions, QA, and unbilled project administration across all delivery roles. Report `(professional-service fee - owned-scope direct cash delivery cost) / total effective hours` separately as cash contribution per effective hour. Count risk once in project economic surplus rather than subtracting it from both measures.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional realization references:** use **$125 per total effective hour** as the normal base target and **$110 per total effective hour** as the stress floor. Flag results from $110 to below $125 for explicit review; reprice or rescope below $110 unless Felix approves a named learning exception. Apply these values only to service-fee realization under the approved definition, review them after the first three closed authorized scopes per module, and do not present them as public hourly prices.

Professional wage and GSA ceiling evidence supports rate plausibility, not launch entitlement. Established-contractor ceiling rates, employee wages, and Wellridge’s realized fixed-fee rate are different measures.

Before any scope is marked internally SOW-ready, complete the [project pricing and cash gate](../templates/internal-pricing-gate.md). A price must expose contribution, effective-hour realization, imputed Felix labor, pass-through, project economic surplus, collection timing, and peak working-capital exposure separately. Passing one measure does not cure failure on another, and the worksheet does not itself authorize an external quote.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contribution application:** calculate core contribution from professional-service fee less owned-scope direct cash delivery cost, before Felix labor and overhead, with pass-through excluded. Use the applicable base target for normal estimating; flag a result between target and stress floor for explicit review; reprice or rescope below the stress floor unless Felix approves a named learning exception. Review the guardrails after the first three closed authorized scopes per module.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Payment and recurrence

- Prefer fixed fees and milestone billing over hourly presentation.
- For government and prime work, follow the contract’s invoicing and payment terms.
- If recurring work is useful, use a 90-day or annual master agreement with bounded quarterly task orders.
- Price research, coordination, and QA into every project, even when Felix initially performs some of it.
- Require adequate working-capital or contract protection before committing material vendor spend.

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED — Pricing practices

- Percentage-of-grant-award compensation
- Contingent fees that violate government-contracting rules
- Unlimited proposals or revisions
- A low retainer for an employee-like outsourced department
- Fronting major pass-through costs without protection
- Calling contribution margin “profit” when Felix labor and overhead remain unpaid
- Publishing these ranges before market validation and public-copy approval
