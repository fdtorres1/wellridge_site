## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Taglines

- `From funding strategy to program delivery.`
- `Funding secured. Programs delivered.`

Neither is final. “Funding secured” can imply an outcome promise if used carelessly; any public use must make clear that Wellridge does not guarantee awards.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Positioning architecture

The message should move in this order:

1. **Category:** Funding & Program Operations
2. **Capability chain:** qualify funding, develop compliant proposals, launch and operate funded programs
3. **Buyer lanes:** nonprofits; established public-sector contractors and selected agencies
4. **Initial proof wedge:** bounded pipeline/proposal work
5. **Growth wedge:** program administration, engagement, coordination, reporting, and closeout
6. **Scope boundary:** nonclinical and nontechnical unless qualified partners own specialized judgment

This avoids presenting the company as either a generic grant writer or a prime-anything intermediary.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Buyer-specific language

### Nonprofits

> Build a qualified funding pipeline, produce compliant proposals, and put the operating systems in place to manage awards.

### Established government contractors

> Add proposal, compliance, stakeholder, reporting, and program-coordination capacity through a bounded, accountable work package.

### Public agencies

> Support selected program-launch, engagement, reporting, and coordination scopes where Wellridge can own real outcomes without claiming technical or clinical authority.

Exact copy remains editable. These are internal message frames, not approved public claims.

## OPEN QUESTION — Clearance workflow

Complete this only in a separately authorized clearance task:

1. Search Texas SOSDirect for the exact name and close variants.
2. Search USPTO records for exact and confusingly similar marks.
3. Search common-law use across the web, state records, industry sources, and professional directories for `Wellridge`, `Well Ridge`, `Wellbridge`, and similar forms.
4. Recheck domains and relevant social handles.
5. Obtain legal review if a potentially material collision appears.

Official starting points:

- [Texas SOSDirect](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/sosda/index.shtml) — currently charges a statutory search fee; preliminary availability is not final name approval.
- [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-information) — exact matching alone is insufficient; confusing similarity can involve sight, sound, meaning, commercial impression, and related services.
