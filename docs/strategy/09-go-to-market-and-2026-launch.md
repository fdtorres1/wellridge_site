# Go-to-market and 2026 launch plan

Last updated: July 12, 2026

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Internal operating plan, not authorization to contact, register, submit, publish, or launch.**

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Channel priority

1. Warm relationships and professional introductions
2. White-label or backend production for existing consultants and proposal shops
3. Established government contractors needing proposal, compliance, or program support
4. APEX counseling, matchmaking, and prime introductions
5. Selected local nonprofits with paid leadership, operating capacity, and real budgets
6. Targeted, highly personalized outreach rather than generic mass volume

This sequence ranks relationship and entry paths, not the number of accounts in the research universe or a fixed allocation of weekly hours.

The 2026 plan does not depend on a direct federal prime award.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Sourcing streams and evidence boundary

Use four internal account types across three opportunity streams:

1. nonprofit direct;
2. consultant/referral partner and established prime; and
3. selective agency watch.

These streams sit beneath the existing two buyer lanes. They do not create additional public offers. The canonical account-status, qualification, scoring, deduplication, and provenance rules are in the [`opportunity-sourcing playbook`](../operations/opportunity-sourcing-playbook.md).

A directory entry, award notice, job posting, supplier portal, or AI-generated inference creates a `RESEARCH_TARGET` or `WATCH_ACCOUNT` at most. It does not prove consulting demand, allowable external spend, buyer authority, timing, or fit. Only a current solicitation or substantive buyer/partner exchange that clears every documented gate may create a `QUALIFIED_OPPORTUNITY`.

**HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Attention allocation:** approximately 60% nonprofit direct, 30% consultant/prime, and 10% agency-watch sourcing effort for six to eight weeks. This is not a fixed weekly-hour commitment. Retain or change it using source-attributed conversations, qualified opportunities, cycle time, proposed value, wins, and founder effort.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Target profiles

### Nonprofit buyer

- Paid executive/advancement/program leadership
- Clear program priorities and access to internal facts
- Budget for professional services
- Realistic funding horizon and decision authority
- Need for a qualified funder pipeline, bounded proposal, or award-operating system

### Prime/consultant partner

- Existing public-sector relationships, vehicle, or past performance
- Recurring proposal, compliance, stakeholder, reporting, or program-coordination gap
- Willingness to define a first-tier work package and references/performance evidence
- Acceptable subcontract terms, payment timing, data access, and margin

### Selective agency buyer

- Narrow administrative, engagement, reporting, or program-launch need
- Procurement path suitable for a new firm or team
- No unsupported technical/clinical prime responsibility
- Contract value and payment structure compatible with Wellridge liquidity

Use the [direct-government opportunity go/no-go rubric](../operations/direct-government-go-no-go-rubric.md) for any named forecast or solicitation. As of July 12, 2026, CroSS-DFW is a `WATCH / PRE-CAPTURE` signal with a likely team-only structure and a completed local [capture packet](../opportunities/cross-dfw-pre-release-capture-brief.md); the closed Workforce Solutions strategic-planning RFP is a strong future archetype but exposes proof/readiness gaps; and Tarrant County F2026147 is a prime `NO-BID` because full-service marketing and in-house creative capability are central.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Disqualification signals

- Wants percentage-of-award compensation or a guaranteed win
- Cannot provide decision-maker access or required inputs
- Treats the firm as unlimited on-call staff for a small fee
- Requires clinical, engineering, construction, software, or other technical authority Wellridge cannot own
- Requires major pass-through spend without cash protection
- Has an impossible timeline, unclear acceptance, or uncontrolled revisions
- Expects improper influence, misrepresentation, or use of confidential information
- Creates an unmanaged conflict with Wright Wellness or another relationship
