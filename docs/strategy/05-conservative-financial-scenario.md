# Conservative hybrid financial scenario

Last updated: July 11, 2026

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — This is the primary planning baseline and a conservative successful path, not a guarantee or a probability-weighted forecast.**

All amounts are nominal planning dollars before Felix’s personal taxes unless stated otherwise. Actual accounting, tax, revenue-recognition, and owner-pay treatment requires professional review.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Measure discipline

- **Recognized revenue** is not the same as cash collected.
- **Project/direct cost** is not the same as total operating cost.
- **Owner compensation + profit pool** includes compensation for Felix’s labor.
- **True profit** is calculated after an imputed market replacement cost for Felix’s strategic, capture, and executive-production work.
- **Award value, billings, SBA receipts, accounting revenue, cash, contribution margin, owner pay, and true profit remain separate.**

| Period | Revenue | Project contractors/direct cost | Fixed non-founder staff | Overhead | Owner compensation + profit pool | Imputed founder replacement pay | True profit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Jul–Dec 2026 | 18 | 3 | 0 | 6 | 9 | 9 | ~0 |
| 2027 / Y1 | 75 | 14 | 0 | 12 | 49 | 60 | (11) |
| 2028 / Y2 | 115 | 22 | 25 | 15 | 53 | 60 | (7) |
| 2029 / Y3 | 165 | 30 | 52 | 18 | 65 | 60 | 5 |
| 2030 / Y4 | 230 | 45 | 70 | 23 | 92 | 65 | 27 |
| 2031 / Y5 | 310 | 65 | 100 | 30 | 115 | 70 | 45 |

Model formulas:

```text
owner compensation + profit pool
= revenue - project/direct cost - fixed non-founder staff - overhead

true profit
= owner compensation + profit pool - imputed founder replacement pay
```

Interpretation:

- Years 1–2 remain economically closer to self-employment than a founder-independent firm.
- Positive economic profit beyond Felix’s labor begins around Year 3 in this model.
- Year-5 owner pool of $115,000 is not $115,000 of profit; the modeled true profit is $45,000 after $70,000 of replacement pay.
- Retained reserves, debt service, taxes, capital expenditure, and distributions are not fully modeled.
