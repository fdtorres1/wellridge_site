# Executive summary

Last updated: July 11, 2026

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — The company logic

**Wellridge Partners** is a working identity for a funding-to-program-operations firm. It is not a generic grant writer, a clinical provider, a technical prime, or a broker that wins work and passes it through. The firm is designed around one capability chain:

```text
Nonprofits:                 Funding pipeline -> Proposal -> Funded-program operations
Government contractors:                        Proposal -> Program operations
Public agencies:                                        -> Select program delivery
```

The working descriptor is **Funding & Program Operations**. Current positioning language, still editable, is:

> Wellridge Partners helps nonprofits and public-sector organizations secure funding, develop compliant proposals, and launch and operate funded programs.

The company has two buyer lanes:

1. **Nonprofit funding and proposal work** — the initial cash engine.
2. **Public-sector proposal and program operations** — the growth engine, initially through established primes and selective subcontracts.

The lanes reuse research, go/no-go analysis, compliance matrices, proposal calendars, narrative/workplan development, budget-to-scope alignment, production QA, implementation planning, deliverable calendars, stakeholder/vendor coordination, reporting, records, and closeout.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — What launches first

Only two offers should be prominent at launch:

| Front door | Buyer problem | Working price hypothesis |
|---|---|---:|
| Nonprofit Funding Pipeline Sprint | “Where should we pursue funding, and what must we fix first?” | About $2,250 pilot; $3,000–$4,000 after proof |
| Proposal & Program Operations Support | “How do we qualify, produce, launch, and govern this bounded scope?” | About $4,000–$8,000 for ordinary proposal operations; $10,000–$25,000 for narrow initial program-launch/compliance work |

Every engagement requires a bounded statement of work. Fixed fees and milestone billing are preferred. Percentage-of-award compensation, unlimited proposals, unlimited revisions, and employee-like small retainers are rejected.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Scope boundary

Wellridge may own administrative, operational, engagement, coordination, compliance, reporting, and closeout work. It may not claim technical or clinical work it cannot responsibly perform.

| Example | Boundary |
|---|---|
| Build a road | **EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED** |
| Manage engagement, reporting, or public education for a road program | **DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — potentially in scope after qualification** |
| Engineer software | **EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED** |
| Coordinate rollout, training, adoption, documentation, and reporting for a software program | **DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — potentially in scope after qualification** |
| Provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical triage, utilization decisions, or clinical supervision | **EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED** |
| Run the nonclinical implementation, outreach, partner, compliance, and reporting backbone for a behavioral-health access program | **DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — potentially in scope with licensed clinical leadership wherever clinical judgment enters** |

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Best initial niche

The strongest initial specialization hypothesis is **nonclinical behavioral-health access and community-program operations**, followed by **workforce and training-program operations**. Public engagement is a useful embedded capability but is not the preferred launch vertical because it can bring evening, travel, event, vendor, and working-capital burdens.

The legal name and top-level brand remain broader until paid evidence supports specialization. A niche is earned through repeated paid work, a reference-ready result, a reusable method, repeat buyers, acceptable margin and payment timing, and work Felix wants to keep doing.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Founder design

Felix should own capture strategy, relationships, opportunity judgment, solution design, client interviews, narrative architecture, partner selection, and final QA. He should not remain the sole owner of deadline memory, attachments, production checklists, routine status chasing, reporting trackers, vendor administration, bookkeeping, or invoicing.

The first consistent support role is a highly organized compliance/project coordinator. Pricing should carry roughly 15–20% for research, coordination, and QA before that support is consistently outsourced.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Financial paths

The **conservative hybrid scenario** is the planning baseline. It is a conservative successful path, not a guarantee or probability-weighted forecast:

| Period | Recognized revenue | Owner compensation + profit pool | True profit after imputed founder replacement pay |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Jul–Dec 2026 | $18,000 | $9,000 | approximately $0 |
| 2027 | $75,000 | $49,000 | $(11,000) |
| 2028 | $115,000 | $53,000 | $(7,000) |
| 2029 | $165,000 | $65,000 | $5,000 |
| 2030 | $230,000 | $92,000 | $27,000 |
| 2031 | $310,000 | $115,000 | $45,000 |

Owner compensation is payment for Felix’s labor plus any residual profit; it is not itself business profit. Under this model, Years 1–2 are economically closer to self-employment, with positive economic profit beyond founder labor beginning around Year 3.

The **$1 million Year-5 scenario** is an aggressive target. It means $1 million recognized during 2031, not cumulative revenue or an annualized exit run rate. It requires four large program contracts or equivalent anchor work, meaningful repeat revenue, six to eight non-founder FTE equivalents, dedicated capture, and substantial working capital. More small grant sprints alone will not produce that scale.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — 2026 validation targets

Illustrative operating targets, not guaranteed conversion benchmarks:

- 40–60 carefully selected organizations, primes, and partners
- 15–20 substantive buyer or partner conversations
- 8–10 credible scopes or proposals
- 3–5 wins depending on fee mix
- about $18,000 recognized revenue and $15,000 collected by December 31 in the baseline

End-of-year success must include more than revenue: two usable case studies, two credible prime/partner relationships, one repeat/expansion client, reusable delivery tools, visible 2027 backlog, and evidence about behavioral-health access versus workforce programming.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Entry sequence

1. Sell bounded proposal/compliance and program-support services to nonprofits and established primes.
2. Build first-tier subcontract past performance and references.
3. Pursue small local or municipal direct-prime scopes where administration, engagement, reporting, or program delivery is central.
4. Expand direct-prime work only after compliance systems, liquidity, partners, and past performance exist.

The 2026 plan does not assume a direct federal prime award.
