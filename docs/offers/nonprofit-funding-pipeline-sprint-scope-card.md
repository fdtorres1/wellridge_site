# Internal scope card — Nonprofit Funding Pipeline Sprint

Last updated: July 12, 2026

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Internal scoping and estimating artifact. It is not approved public copy, a standing quote, or authorization to sell, contact, publish, or launch.**

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Offer boundary

- This remains one of two visible front-door offers.
- It produces a dated, prioritized funding-pursuit plan; it does not promise eligibility, an application, an award, or continued monitoring.
- Use a fixed fee, explicit assumptions, and one bounded revision cycle.
- Exclude percentage-of-award compensation, contingent compensation, unlimited work, and employee-like ongoing support.
- AI may assist research and drafting; humans verify material facts, rankings, and final delivery.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Buyer, problem, and outcome

| Element | Internal definition |
|---|---|
| Best-fit buyer | Nonprofit with paid leadership, one defined program or funding priority, access to verified internal facts, realistic decision authority, operating capacity, and budget for bounded professional services |
| Buyer problem | Scattered funder names or reactive applications without a sourced priority list, pursuit calendar, and explicit readiness-gap view |
| Outcome | Research-qualified funder profiles ranked against agreed criteria, a 6- or 12-month pursuit calendar, and specific readiness/evidence actions |
| Success boundary | Client can decide what to pursue, cultivate, monitor, or exclude before spending on proposals |
| Not an outcome | Guaranteed award, final eligibility opinion, funder introduction, submitted proposal, comprehensive development strategy, or ongoing outsourced department |

### Research-qualified funder profile

This term is deliberately different from a Wellridge `QUALIFIED_OPPORTUNITY`. A profile counts toward the agreed total only when it records:

1. plausible mission, program, population, and geography fit;
2. dated evidence of relevant giving, comparable recipients, or an explicit funding interest;
3. the known application or relationship path, with invitation-only or uncertain status flagged;
4. typical award size where authoritative information exists, otherwise `UNKNOWN`;
5. known deadline/cycle and official application/contact path where available, otherwise `UNKNOWN`;
6. eligibility, conflict, competition, or readiness risks; and
7. source URL and access date for every material external fact.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Included scope

- One legal organization or fiscally sponsored project
- One primary program or funding priority
- One agreed service geography and target population
- Agreed funder classes, exclusions, source depth, and calendar horizon
- One kickoff and written criteria lock
- 20–30 research-qualified funder profiles
- Comparable-giving or stated-interest evidence where available
- Fit, risk, exclusion, and readiness-gap analysis
- Typical award sizes, deadlines, and official application/contact paths where available
- Priority tiers with short rationale
- One 6- or 12-month pursuit calendar
- Recommended next actions and evidence gaps
- Source register with URLs and access dates
- One editable working file, one concise findings summary, and one delivery readout

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED

- Proposal drafting, editing, production, submission, or portal registration
- Funder outreach, introductions, cultivation, lobbying, or representation
- Guaranteed eligibility, funding, ranking accuracy, or award probability
- Percentage-of-award or success-fee compensation
- Donor prospecting, sponsorship sales, capital-campaign design, or a comprehensive development plan
- Ongoing monitoring or calendar administration after the delivery date
- Multiple unrelated programs, entities, or geographies in one narrow sprint
- Legal, tax, accounting, audit, clinical, or other professional opinions
- Database acquisition or redistribution that violates license terms
- PHI, beneficiary-level records, credentials, banking data, or unnecessary personal information
- Unlimited meetings, revisions, substitutions, or urgent proposal rescue

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Required client inputs

The schedule starts only after the client provides:

- executed agreement and cleared deposit;
- one accountable decision-maker and one consolidated feedback owner;
- verified legal/entity status and any fiscal-sponsor arrangement;
- current mission, program description, population, geography, and operating model;
- one primary funding priority and realistic pursuit horizon;
- current program budget or credible amount sought;
- current/recent funders, pending applications, declines, and excluded or conflicted funders;
- existing grant calendar and relevant prior proposals or award documents;
- evidence, outcomes, partnerships, credentials, and readiness constraints affecting fit;
- minimum/maximum useful award size and invitation-only tolerance; and
- approval for any nonpublic material permitted in an AI-assisted workflow.

Incomplete or materially changing inputs pause the schedule and may require a change order.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Timeline and milestones

| Variant | Working assumption | Target after all start conditions |
|---|---|---:|
| Narrow pilot | 20 profiles; one program family; one geography; 6-month calendar | 10 business days |
| Standard | 25–30 profiles; deeper source coverage and/or 12-month calendar | 15 business days |

| Milestone | Output |
|---|---|
| 1. Start and criteria lock | Inputs, kickoff, exclusions, scoring criteria, source depth, and horizon confirmed |
| 2. Research screen | Longlist researched; duplicates removed; material ineligibility and uncertainty flagged |
| 3. Prioritization and QA | Profiles completed; priority rationale applied; calendar, citations, and calculations checked |
| 4. Delivery and readout | Working file, findings summary, and decision-oriented readout delivered |
| 5. Revision and closeout | One consolidated revision; acceptance and unresolved items recorded; actual hours/cost/rework logged |

Client-caused delays move milestone dates. Rush work requires a separate capacity, quality, and pricing decision.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Meeting and revision caps

- One kickoff up to 45 minutes
- One findings/readout meeting up to 60 minutes
- Routine coordination otherwise asynchronous
- One consolidated written revision round received within five business days
- One reprioritization pass and up to three preference-driven funder substitutions
- Wellridge factual defects or profiles that fail locked criteria corrected outside the preference cap
- New program, geography, funder class, horizon, or materially changed strategy treated as scope change

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — QA and acceptance

### Delivery roles

| Role | Owned work |
|---|---|
| Felix | criteria, fit judgment, prioritization, client readout, final QA |
| Research analyst | discovery, source capture, profiles, comparable-giving evidence |
| Compliance/project coordinator | inputs, document control, completeness, link/date/calendar QA, closeout |

### Internal QA

- Prefer official/primary sources for eligibility, deadlines, and application paths; label secondary sources.
- Recheck time-sensitive deadlines and application status before delivery.
- Use `UNKNOWN` when a fact cannot be verified; never substitute AI inference for evidence.
- Identify client-supplied facts for client confirmation.
- Verify duplicate control, source links, date logic, ranking rationale, and calendar reconciliation.

### Acceptance standard

The deliverable is ready when:

- the agreed profile count meets locked criteria;
- required fields are complete or marked `UNKNOWN`;
- every material external fact has a source and access date;
- priority tiers and exclusions have stated rationale;
- the calendar reconciles to the delivered profiles;
- agreed evidence gaps and next actions are included; and
- files open in the agreed format with no known Wellridge defect.

Acceptance does not mean that a funder has confirmed eligibility or interest.

The reusable [Funding Pipeline Sprint delivery workbook](../templates/nonprofit-funding-pipeline-delivery-workbook.md) implements the profile, source, uncertainty, calendar, QA, and acceptance controls. It is an internal sample, not evidence of completed delivery or a client-ready artifact without project-specific review.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Pricing and payment

| Item | Current internal hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Narrow pilot | approximately **$2,250** |
| Standard after proof | approximately **$3,000–$4,000** |
| Outside research/tool cost | approximately **$300–$800** |
| Contribution before Felix labor and overhead | approximately **75–90%**, subject to reconciliation below |
| Payment | **60% deposit / 40% on final delivery** |

Build the fee from:

```text
estimated labor value
+ outside research, coordination, QA, and tool cost
+ any required risk allowance
= proposed fixed fee
```

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contingency and stress method:** build estimated labor value from role hours after applying 20% planned contingency once. Do not add another contingency fee line. Require a named stress scenario; if credible evidence is unavailable, use stress hours of at least 125% of the contingency-loaded plan. The stress case tests viability and is not an automatic client charge.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional overhead proxies:** use 16% of professional-service fee in the base case and 25% in stress. Exclude pass-through and costs already recorded as direct delivery, fixed staff, Felix replacement labor, or risk. Stress is an internal project-pricing sensitivity, not an automatic client charge or substitute for the business-level runway review.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional project-economic-surplus references:** require at least 10% of professional-service fee in base and at least zero in stress after direct delivery cost, fixed-staff allocation, Felix replacement labor, overhead, and risk. A shortfall requires repricing, rescoping, or a named Felix-approved learning exception. Do not call this proxy accounting profit or cash.

Do not select a range endpoint until the scope-specific estimate reconciles total effective hours, direct costs, the provisional $125/hour base realization target / $110/hour stress floor, and the provisional 80% base contribution target / 75% stress floor. Payment is never contingent on an award.

### Economic pressure test

The current pilot assumptions do not reconcile across their full ranges:

| Fee and outside cost | Contribution before Felix labor/overhead | Maximum hours at $125 base target | Maximum hours at $110 stress floor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $2,250 fee / $300 cost | 86.7% | 18.0 | about 20.5 |
| $2,250 fee / $800 cost | 64.4% | 18.0 | about 20.5 |
| $3,000 fee / $800 cost | 73.3% | 24.0 | about 27.3 |

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional realization guardrail:** calculate the hour boundary from the full professional-service fee, not contribution dollars. A $2,250 fee supports 18.0 total effective hours at the $125 base target or about 20.5 hours at the $110 stress floor. Direct cost is tested separately through the contribution guardrail and project economics.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contribution guardrail:** use an 80% base target and 75% stress floor. At $2,250, outside cost must stay at or below $450 to reach the base target and $562.50 to remain at the stress floor.

The narrow-pilot labor hypothesis of 16–20 raw hours becomes 19.2–24.0 planned hours after the approved 20% contingency and 24.0–30.0 fallback-stress hours when better stress evidence is unavailable. A $2,250 fee supports only 18.0 hours at the $125 base target and about 20.5 at the $110 stress floor. Therefore the pilot requires at least one of: a lower direct-cost cap, smaller/deeper-defined scope, higher price, demonstrated delivery efficiency, or a named Felix-approved learning exception. Do not solve the mismatch by weakening source verification or QA.

The separate operating assumption that 15–20% of fees should cover research, coordination, and QA equals only $337.50–$450 at the pilot price. Clarify whether tools and contractor delivery are separate direct-cost categories before approving a quote.

Use the [project pricing and cash gate](../templates/internal-pricing-gate.md) before marking any version internally SOW-ready. The selected fee must apply the provisional contribution, realization, contingency/stress, overhead, and project-economic-surplus controls and separately address cash and capacity; the worksheet does not authorize an external quote.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Qualification gates

Proceed to a decision-ready SOW only when evidence supports:

1. current need for prioritization rather than an immediate proposal rescue;
2. one responsible buyer and approval path;
3. credible professional-services budget and deposit path;
4. one primary program, geography, and realistic horizon;
5. access to verified facts and existing funder/application history;
6. acceptance of a dated research screen and bounded deliverables;
7. compatibility with capacity, data controls, and nonclinical boundaries; and
8. a specific next action with owner and date.

A sourced-account score cannot clear these gates.

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED — No-go gates

Decline, pause, or route elsewhere when a buyer:

- wants guaranteed funding or percentage-of-award compensation;
- primarily needs an imminent application rather than a funding pipeline;
- cannot identify a decision-maker, priority program, credible budget, or required inputs;
- expects unlimited research, meetings, updates, substitutions, or administration;
- requires multiple unrelated programs or geographies without expanded scope and price;
- asks Wellridge to make legal, tax, audit, clinical, or technical determinations;
- requires sensitive data beyond the approved security posture;
- rejects source/uncertainty labeling or asks for misrepresentation;
- creates an unmanaged conflict or related-party concern; or
- has timeline, acceptance, or payment terms that make quality or contribution uneconomic.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Data, AI, and human review

- Use minimum-necessary, organization-level information.
- Do not place PHI, beneficiary records, credentials, banking data, or unnecessary personal information in general research or AI tools.
- Require approval before using nonpublic client material in an AI-assisted workflow.
- AI may assist discovery, summarization, comparison, deduplication, drafting, and checklist review.
- AI may not independently establish eligibility, deadlines, grant sizes, contact facts, legal meaning, fit, ranking, or final recommendations.
- Humans verify all material facts, citations, calculations, profiles, and calendar entries.
- Felix remains accountable for final QA; the client remains accountable for client facts and pursuit approval.
- Any sensitive-data need triggers separate security, retention, access, and contract review.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Optional bounded expansion

After acceptance, separately scope only what evidence supports:

- another sprint for a distinct program, geography, or funder class;
- a readiness/evidence-gap task with explicit deliverables;

These remain expansions or Offer 2 modules, not new front doors or unlimited retainers.
