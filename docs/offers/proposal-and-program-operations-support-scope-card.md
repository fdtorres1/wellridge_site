# Internal scope card — Proposal & Program Operations Support

Last updated: July 12, 2026

**Status: HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Internal scoping and estimating artifact. It is not approved public copy, a standing quote, proof of current capacity, or authorization to sell, contact, register, submit, publish, or launch.**

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Category boundary

This is one buyer-facing category with two bounded modules:

1. **Proposal operations** for a defined pursuit; or
2. **Program launch and compliance** for a defined award, contract, or task.

Every SOW must identify one primary module. Do not combine proposal writing, production, implementation, and ongoing operations into an ambiguous retainer. Expansion requires a task order, amended SOW, or separate scope.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Shared qualification gates

Proceed to a decision-ready SOW only when evidence supports:

1. one responsible buyer, approval path, and contracting/procurement path;
2. a current problem, timing window, and bounded owned work package;
3. credible budget and payment terms;
4. access to controlling documents and verified client facts;
5. named subject-matter, budget, legal/contract, and final approvers as applicable;
6. explicit client, Wellridge, partner, and specialist responsibilities;
7. delivery capacity, contribution, cash, data, security, conflict, and insurance fit; and
8. a specific next action with owner and date.

A sourced-account score, award notice, supplier portal, or public solicitation history cannot clear these gates by itself.

# Module A — Proposal operations

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Buyer, problem, and outcome

| Element | Internal definition |
|---|---|
| Best-fit buyer | Nonprofit or established contractor with a live, qualified pursuit, complete solicitation, accountable response lead, subject-matter access, and budget for bounded support |
| Buyer problem | Requirements, inputs, narrative, workplan, budget, partners, attachments, or production lack a controlled plan and accountable owners |
| Outcome | A compliant, decision-ready response package for the sections and production responsibilities explicitly assigned to Wellridge |
| Success boundary | Mandatory requirements are controlled, inputs and decisions are visible, assigned content is complete, and final QA exceptions are documented before client-authorized submission |
| Not an outcome | Guaranteed award, independent eligibility/legal opinion, unsupported technical content, unlimited rewrites, or Wellridge silently owning the client's submission authority |

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Scope variants

| Variant | Typical owned work | Current internal price hypothesis |
|---|---|---:|
| Compliance/editing/production | requirement extraction, matrix, calendar, editing, assembly, final QA | approximately **$3,000–$5,000** |
| Substantive writing/coordination | above plus interviews, assigned narrative/workplan development, partner/input coordination, budget-to-scope cross-check | approximately **$6,000–$12,000** |
| Complex state, multi-agency, or federal pursuit | extensive sections, partners, volumes, technical dependencies, or unusual production | separately scoped; may exceed ordinary range |

The broader ordinary-launch hypothesis remains approximately **$4,000–$8,000**. Choose a variant from actual responsibility; do not average these ranges into a quote.

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED

- Award guarantee, win-rate promise, improper influence, lobbying, or prohibited contingent compensation
- Legal interpretation, certification, accounting approval, indirect-rate approval, or price certification
- Invented client facts, technical claims, credentials, past performance, partner commitments, or letters
- Clinical, engineering, software, cybersecurity, evaluation-method, or other specialist authority without a qualified named owner
- Unlimited sections, revisions, meetings, interviews, partners, attachments, or portal work
- Credential sharing through insecure channels or unnecessary retention of portal access
- Submission under Wellridge authority unless the contract, client approval, security method, and responsibility are explicit
- Daily capture management or employee-like availability outside the defined pursuit

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Required client inputs

- Executed agreement and applicable deposit/milestone authorization
- Complete solicitation, attachments, amendments, Q&A, incumbent/background files, and internal deadline
- Client go decision, response lead, final approver, and consolidated feedback owner
- Verified organizational facts, legal name, registrations/certifications, resumes, past performance, and references
- Approved solution, technical/clinical subject-matter owners, partners/subcontractors, and responsibility split
- Approved pricing assumptions, cost inputs, budget policy, and finance/accounting approver
- Required forms, representations, letters, signatures, and portal owner
- Brand/template files and production rules
- Timely review access and written client approval of all final external content

Late or changing inputs move dates, reduce scope, trigger rush/change pricing, or create a no-go.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Schedule, meetings, and revisions

- One solicitation and one response configuration per SOW.
- Compliance/editing/production ordinarily begins at least 10 business days before the agreed internal final date.
- Substantive writing/coordination ordinarily begins at least 20 business days before the agreed internal final date.
- Complex work receives a separate calendar and capacity review.
- One kickoff up to 60 minutes; status cadence and interview count stated in the SOW.
- One consolidated client revision round per assigned complete draft, plus correction of Wellridge defects.
- Addenda received after criteria lock trigger impact review and possible change control.
- Client feedback must be consolidated, authoritative, and returned by the agreed date.
- Wellridge's internal final precedes the external deadline by an agreed submission buffer.

These thresholds are hypotheses, not promises. A short deadline never waives QA, client approval, or scope control.

| Role | Owned work |
|---|---|
| Felix | opportunity judgment, response architecture, interviews, high-value narrative, partner decisions, final QA |
| Compliance/project coordinator | matrix, calendar, inputs, status, document control, production checklist, versioning, handoff |

Acceptance requires completion of the assigned matrix rows and deliverables, a reconciled version manifest, documented exceptions, and client approval. Award outcome is not an acceptance criterion.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Pricing pressure test

| Fee and outside delivery cost | Contribution before Felix labor/overhead |
|---|---:|
| $3,000 / $750 | 75.0% |
| $3,000 / $2,000 | 33.3% |
| $4,000 / $750 | 81.3% |
| $4,000 / $2,000 | 50.0% |
| $8,000 / $2,000 | 75.0% |

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional Proposal contribution guardrail:** use a 70% base target and 65% stress floor. At a $4,000 fee, outside delivery cost must not exceed $1,200 to reach the base target or $1,400 to remain at the stress floor. The broader **65–85%** planning range cannot coexist with every fee/cost combination. Use two separate tests:

```text
service-fee realization = fee / total effective hours

cash contribution per effective hour
= (fee - outside delivery cost) / total effective hours
```

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Realization guardrail:** the first formula is the primary service-fee realization measure. Use $125 per total effective hour as the provisional base target and $110 as the provisional stress floor. Flag results from $110 to below $125 for explicit review; reprice or rescope below $110 unless Felix approves a named learning exception. “Total effective hours” includes discovery, research, production, meetings, coordination, revisions, QA, and unbilled project administration across every delivery role.

The second formula shows what remains per hour after outside direct delivery cost and has no approved numerical threshold. It is a separate KPI; do not apply the $125/$110 service-fee references to it.

Do not subtract the risk/capital allowance inside cash contribution per effective hour. Count risk once in the separate project-economic-surplus calculation alongside Felix replacement labor, fixed-staff allocation, and overhead. Test all measures against actual responsibility, deadline, coordination, revision, QA, founder labor, overhead, and cash exposure. White-label consultant and prime-subcontract tasks require their own wholesale/contract economics; direct-client ranges do not automatically apply.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional contingency and stress method:** apply 20% planned contingency once to role hours and do not add it again as a fee line. Require a named stress scenario for revision, input, coordination, specialist, production, delay, and defect risk. When credible evidence is unavailable, use stress hours of at least 125% of the contingency-loaded plan. The stress case is an internal viability test against the $110 realization floor and the other financial gates, not an automatic client charge.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional overhead proxies:** use 16% of professional-service fee in base and 25% in stress. Exclude pass-through and amounts already classified as direct delivery cost, fixed-staff allocation, Felix replacement labor, or risk. Stress is an internal sensitivity, not an automatic client charge or a worst-case company-overhead forecast.

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional project-economic-surplus references:** require at least 10% of professional-service fee in base and at least zero in stress after direct delivery cost, fixed-staff allocation, Felix replacement labor, overhead, and risk. Reprice or rescope below either reference unless Felix approves a named learning exception. This is not accounting profit or cash.

# Module B — Program launch and compliance

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Buyer, problem, and outcome

| Element | Internal definition |
|---|---|
| Best-fit buyer | Funded nonprofit, established prime, or selective agency with a defined award/task, accountable program owner, source documents, and a bounded administrative operating gap |
| Buyer problem | Award requirements, roles, dates, partners, vendors, reporting, records, or closeout lack an integrated control system |
| Outcome | A usable launch and governance package for the administrative/program-operations responsibilities assigned to Wellridge |
| Success boundary | Owners can operate the first defined period with visible deliverables, decisions, risks, records, and escalation |
| Not an outcome | Clinical/technical delivery, full outsourced department, daily case management, participant chasing, 24/7 coverage, or indefinite program management |

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Included deliverables

Select only the rows assigned in the SOW:

| Deliverable | Minimum content | Acceptance boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Award/contract digest | scope, dates, deliverables, clauses, budget categories, reporting, records, flow-downs, unresolved review items | legal/contract interpretation remains with authorized reviewer |
| Implementation plan | workstreams, milestones, dependencies, first 30/60/90-day cadence | accountable client owner approves feasibility |
| Governance and escalation map | roles, decision rights, meeting cadence, escalation, partner/specialist authority | clinical/technical judgment assigned outside Wellridge where applicable |
| Compliance/deliverable calendar | source requirement, owner, evidence, due date, review/acceptance path | reconciles to controlling documents |
| Spend/milestone tracker | approved categories, budget-to-activity mapping, invoice/milestone visibility | accounting, allowability, and certification remain client-owned |
| Reporting framework | measures, definitions, sources, owner, cadence, QA, aggregation, narrative inputs | evaluation design and clinical outcomes require qualified owner |
| Stakeholder/partner plan | audiences, responsibilities, decisions, communication cadence, risks | outreach execution included only if expressly scoped |
| Vendor/subrecipient control | responsibility, deliverables, inputs, flow-down checklist, invoice/evidence path | procurement/legal authority remains client-owned |
| Records and closeout structure | file taxonomy, evidence index, version/retention responsibilities, closeout checklist | security and retention rules approved by client |
| Handoff | owner training, open-risk log, operating cadence, next-task recommendations | ongoing administration excluded unless separately ordered |

## EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE / REJECTED

- Therapy, diagnosis, clinical triage, utilization decisions, treatment plans, clinical supervision, or crisis-line operations
- Engineering, construction, software development, cybersecurity authority, accounting certification, or legal advice
- Participant eligibility/case management, daily reminder loops, or unbounded partner/vendor chasing
- Evaluation design or causal outcome claims without a qualified evaluator
- PHI or sensitive-data processing before contract, BAA where applicable, security, access, retention, and incident controls are approved
- Automatic routing or preference for Wright Wellness or any related party
- Major pass-through/vendor spend without funded terms, retained margin, and adequate liquidity
- Prime responsibility where Wellridge lacks technical capacity, past performance, systems, insurance, or working capital
- Meetings, travel, events, reports, partners, vendors, or operating months beyond the SOW caps

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Required client inputs

- Executed agreement and authorized milestone/payment terms
- Final award, contract/task order, amendments, budget, proposal, workplan, and representations
- Accountable program owner, contract/legal reviewer, finance approver, reporting owner, and final decision-maker
- Approved partner/vendor/subrecipient agreements and contacts
- Existing policies, systems, calendars, templates, data definitions, records rules, and known audit findings
- Approved outcomes/measures and qualified clinical/technical/evaluation owners where relevant
- Data inventory and classification, approved access method, security/BAA/retention requirements, and minimum-necessary rule
- Working-capital and vendor-payment plan when Wellridge would incur or manage direct cost

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Scope caps and schedule

- One award, contract, or task order and one defined organizational unit per initial SOW.
- Narrow launch task: approximately 4–8 weeks and one 30/60/90-day design horizon.
- Mature/larger task: separately calendared; may use an 8–12-week implementation period or bounded recurring task orders.
- One kickoff up to 60 minutes and a stated weekly/biweekly decision cadence.
- Named stakeholder, partner, vendor, deliverable, and report counts.
- One consolidated review round per major system/deliverable plus correction of Wellridge defects.
- Daily operations after handoff are excluded unless a funded task order names owners, capacity, controls, and duration.

## HYPOTHESIS TO TEST — Pricing and payment

| Item | Current internal hypothesis |
|---|---:|
| Initial narrow task | approximately **$10,000–$25,000** |
| Mature/larger task | approximately **$30,000–$40,000+** |
| Outside delivery cost on initial task | approximately **$3,000–$10,000** |
| Contribution before Felix labor and overhead | approximately **45–70%** |
| Payment | milestone or monthly invoicing as the governing contract permits |

**DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Provisional Program Launch contribution guardrail:** use a 55% base target and 50% stress floor.

| Fee and outside delivery cost | Contribution before Felix labor/overhead |
|---|---:|
| $10,000 / $3,000 | 70.0% |
| $10,000 / $10,000 | 0.0% |
| $25,000 / $10,000 | 60.0% |
| $30,000 / $10,000 | 66.7% |

The ranges describe different scopes, not interchangeable endpoints. A $10,000 task cannot carry $10,000 of direct cost. At that fee, owned-scope direct cash delivery cost must not exceed $4,500 to reach the provisional base target or $5,000 to remain at the stress floor. Reconcile each SOW to role hours, retained value, subcontract/vendor terms, payment timing, working capital, and founder capacity. Do not front material vendor cost without protection.

The reusable [program-launch compliance-calendar template](../templates/program-launch-compliance-calendar.md) implements source, authority, 30/60/90-day, deliverable, trigger, metric, vendor, data, acceptance, and closeout controls. Use the [project pricing and cash gate](../templates/internal-pricing-gate.md) for either Offer 2 module before internal SOW readiness. Neither template authorizes external release or certifies compliance.

## DECIDED / WORKING DECISION — Shared change control, data, AI, and human review

- State all owned work, inputs, meetings, revisions, travel, data, records, approvals, and acceptance in the SOW.
- Treat new sections, addenda, deliverables, audiences, partners, systems, reporting periods, or operating months as impact/change events.
- AI may assist extraction, matrices, research, drafting, comparison, checklist review, and production.
- AI does not independently interpret law/contracts, make clinical/technical judgments, approve costs, certify facts, submit, or provide final authority.
- Humans verify controlling requirements, client facts, calculations, citations, external content, and final deliverables.
- Client and named specialists retain final approval and their regulated/technical responsibilities.
- Felix owns final Wellridge QA; the coordinator owns control-plane completeness and version integrity.
